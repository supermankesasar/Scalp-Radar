# R11.2 Impulse Exhaustion Radar (28 Nov 2026)
User approved implementation after exploratory Nov 28 Lite pattern audit (EXPERIMENT_RESULTS.md). Keep R08 dashboard default, R11/R11.1 unchanged in formulas. Cache-only shadow module integrated into R11.1 decision cycle; no new network calls or subscriptions. SCANNER_R112=1 via Windows launcher.
- Impulse: current 1m quote RVOL >=1.5 (median prior20), absolute rolling ret5 >=.30%, aligned flow5 >=.10. Direction hypothesis is inverse, LONG after selling impulse, SHORT after buying impulse. Same-direction and inverse controls recorded at identical actual decision cutoff, separate cohorts.
- PREPARE first, no same-candle READY; wait up to five subsequent minutes, require consecutive observed live cycle, cancel opposite impulse or gap. Recovery: signed ret1 >=.03%, signed flow1 >=.10, plus reclaim previous high/low OR rejection wick >=.4 of candle range. Wick proxy is not proof of absorption; thresholds provisional, not derived from a validated model.
- READY requires current side-specific bid/ask from R11.1, spread <=.10%, immediate chase <=.30%, adverse drift >=-.15%, distance from impulse anchor <=max(.30%,1.5 ATR1m%). Retry missing book within same deadline. Monitor 15 minutes, invalidation adverse max(.4%, ATR). Impulse cooldown 15m per symbol. Gap cancellation is conservative. State RAM only, cooldown restored from ledger on restart; no fabricated backfill.
- Outcome cohort controls at impulse and confirmed entry at recovery are distinct. Both use first fully future minute open after actual decision; 5m and 15m signed endpoint plus 15m FIXED policy, full window MFE/MAE, explicit UNOBSERVED. Control entries are theoretical, not executable alerts and not gated by spread. No actual trade. Cost sensitivities .10/.20/.35 percentage points. Confirmed and controls differ in timing/selection; compare paired controls directly, not infer causal benefit from selected subset. No holdout performance or proof yet.
